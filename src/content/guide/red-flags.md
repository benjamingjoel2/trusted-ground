## Stop and check if you see any of these

- **Payment only by bank transfer, cryptocurrency or a money-transfer service**, or a request to pay a personal account. Consumer-protection agencies warn that these methods are hard or impossible to reverse.
- **The payee does not match the company.** The name on the bank details differs from the company name on the contract.
- **Pressure to pay now**: "only one place left", a price that expires within hours.
- **No written terms.** No booking conditions, no cancellation terms, no itinerary with named suppliers.
- **No verifiable address or registration.** The company cannot give a licence number, or the number does not match the register.
- **Membership logos you cannot confirm** with the body itself.
- **A price far below other operators** for the same lodges, permits and season, with no explanation.
- **Copied content.** Photographs or text that also appear on other companies' websites.
- **Contact only through a messaging app or a free email address**, with no company domain.

## What to do

Check each point against the [before-you-pay checklist](/checklist/). If the company cannot answer in writing, do not pay. If you think you have been targeted by fraud, report it to the fraud or consumer authority in your country.

Being listed on Trusted Ground means a company passed the checks for its [tier](/verification/). It does not replace these checks for your own booking.
