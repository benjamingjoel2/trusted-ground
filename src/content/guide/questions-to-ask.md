Ask in writing, keep the answers, and compare them with the contract you are asked to sign.

## The company

1. What is your full legal name, registered address and registration or licence number? Which authority issued it?
2. Are you the company that will run my trip on the ground, or will another company do that? If so, which one?
3. Which trade bodies are you a member of, and what are the membership numbers?

## The trip

4. Which lodges, hotels and activities are confirmed, and which are "or similar"?
5. Who are the guides? Are they licensed, where that is required, and what languages do they speak?
6. What is included in the price, and what is not (park fees, permits, tips, transfers, internal flights)?
7. What happens if the weather, a park closure or a road condition changes the plan?

## The money

8. How much is the deposit, when is the balance due, and how can I pay?
9. What do I get back if I cancel, and when do the charges change?
10. What do I get back if you cancel, and how quickly?
11. Is my money protected if your company fails? By which scheme?

## On the ground

12. Who do I call during the trip, and is the line answered at night?
13. What insurance do you hold, and what does it cover?
14. What do you need from me: passport details, insurance, medical information?

A good operator answers each question plainly, in writing, and is happy to put the answers in the contract. Print the [before-you-pay checklist](/checklist/) to work through them.
