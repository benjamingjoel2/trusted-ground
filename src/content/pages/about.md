Trusted Ground publishes practical travel stories and destination guides: when to go, how things work on the ground, and how to book a trip safely with a local operator.

## Who publishes it

Trusted Ground is published by **Tourground**, which works with local travel companies around the world. Some travel brands are owned by Tourground's founders. The [publisher disclosure](/disclosure/) sets out the relationship.

## How we write

- **Sourced.** Every article, destination guide and answer lists its sources. We prefer official sources: national tourism boards, park authorities, government travel advice and the law itself.
- **Dated.** Every page shows when it was published and when it was last reviewed. Rules, fees and travel advice change, so check the sources before you book.
- **No invented facts.** We do not publish made-up statistics, reviews, quotes or prices. If we cannot source something, we leave it out.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell trips, take bookings or handle payments.
- We do not publish reviews, ratings, rankings or testimonials.
