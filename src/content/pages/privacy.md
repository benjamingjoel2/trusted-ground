This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use Trusted Ground (trustedground.tourground.com). The site is built so that it needs very little personal data: there are no accounts, no forms, no comments, no advertising trackers and no cookies.

## Who is responsible

The controller of personal data processed through this site is **Tourground**, the publisher of Trusted Ground. You can reach us at the email address in the Contact section at the end of this page.

## What we process, why, and on what basis

**1. Visit statistics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views in aggregate. According to Vercel, it does not use cookies; a visitor is identified by a hash created from the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours; and each data point may include the time, the page address, the referring page, filtered query parameters, an approximate location (country, region, city), device type, operating system and browser. We use these statistics only to understand which pages are read and to improve the site.
*Legal basis:* our legitimate interest in running and improving a public reference site (GDPR and UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f); LGPD Article 7, IX). We have assessed that this minimal, aggregated, cookie-free measurement does not override your interests.

**2. Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical request data (such as IP address, time, requested address and browser details) to deliver pages and protect the service against abuse.
*Legal basis:* legitimate interest in operating a secure website.

**3. Emails you send us.** If you email us, we process your email address, name if given, and the content of your message to reply, and to handle any correction request.
*Legal basis:* legitimate interest in answering you; where your message concerns a company record, also our legal obligation to handle data-protection requests.

We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use it for automated decision-making.

## Who receives it

- **Vercel Inc.** (hosting and analytics), acting as our processor.
- **Our email provider**, acting as our processor, for messages you send us.

Vercel is based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU, the UK or Brazil, we rely on the safeguards the law provides for that transfer, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- Analytics: aggregated statistics only; Vercel discards the visitor hash after 24 hours.
- Server logs: for the period set by our hosting provider for security and operations.
- Emails: for as long as needed to deal with your message, and then for up to two years so that we can show how a correction or removal request was handled, unless the law requires longer.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data:

- **EU and UK (GDPR, UK GDPR):** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.
- **Brazil (LGPD):** confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and the right to petition the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).
- **California and other US states with privacy laws:** the right to know, to delete, to correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

To use any of these rights, email us. We will answer within the time the applicable law allows (one month under the GDPR and UK GDPR, which can be extended in some cases). We may need to confirm your identity first.

## Children

This site is a reference for adults planning travel. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we update the "last reviewed" date at the top of the page. Material changes will be described on this page.
