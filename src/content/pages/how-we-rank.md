## Where rank comes from

Every ranked list on Trusted Ground uses the `rank` value for each country in the Tourground directory feed. This site does not compute, adjust or reorder rank. If the feed says a company is No. 2 in Kenya, it is No. 2 in Kenya here.

Tourground describes its ranking as earned, not paid, and says it measures merit from evidence and from performance on bookings made through Tourground. The method belongs to Tourground; this site publishes the result.

## Lists by niche

Rank is set per country, so lists by niche (for example, safari) are grouped by country. Within each country, companies appear in that country's rank order. We never merge countries into a single order, because the feed gives no rank across countries.

## What can never change rank

- **Payment.** Paid plans, including Platinum, do not change rank. Paid placements, when they exist, appear only in a separate box labelled "Advertisement feature" at the top of a page, and never inside a ranked list.
- **Ownership.** Founder-owned brands follow the same ranking rules as every other company.
- **Editorial preference.** The Trusted Ground desk does not move companies up or down.

## Who is eligible

Only companies the directory marks verified, or marks as founder-owned, are listed. See [How verification works](/verification/).
