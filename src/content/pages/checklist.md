title: The before-you-pay checklist
description: One page to vet any local tour operator before paying a deposit. Print it, work through it, and keep the answers in writing.
kind: checklist
published: 2026-11-08
reviewed: 2026-11-08
status: reviewed
sources:
  - url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoid-scams-when-you-travel
    label: "US Federal Trade Commission: Avoid scams when you travel"
items:
  - text: The company's legal name, address and registration or licence number are in writing.
    detail: Ask which authority issued the licence.
    source: { url: "https://cadastur.turismo.gov.br/", label: "Example register: Cadastur (Brazil)" }
    guide: licences
  - text: I checked the licence number on the issuing authority's own website.
    detail: The name on the register matches the name on the contract.
    source: { url: "https://www.tourismauthority.go.ke/", label: "Example authority: Tourism Regulatory Authority (Kenya)" }
    guide: licences
  - text: Any trade association membership is confirmed in that body's own directory.
    source: { url: "https://www.abta.com/", label: "ABTA (UK travel association)" }
    guide: memberships
  - text: I know whether this company runs the trip on the ground (DMC) or resells another company's trip (agency), and which company is the organiser.
    source: { url: "https://www.admei.org/", label: "ADMEI: what a DMC is" }
    guide: dmc-or-agency
  - text: I have the written booking conditions, including cancellation terms for both sides.
    source: { url: "https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2302/oj", label: "Directive (EU) 2015/2302, pre-contractual information (Article 5)" }
    guide: questions-to-ask
  - text: The deposit amount, what it secures and the balance date are in writing.
    source: { url: "https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2302/oj", label: "Directive (EU) 2015/2302 on package travel" }
    guide: deposits-and-payment-protection
  - text: I know what I get back, and how fast, if the operator cancels.
    detail: Under EU package travel law, organisers must refund within 14 days of terminating a package.
    source: { url: "https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2302/oj", label: "Directive (EU) 2015/2302, Article 12" }
    guide: deposits-and-payment-protection
  - text: I know whether my payment is protected if the company fails, and by which scheme.
    source: { url: "https://www.caa.co.uk/atol-protection/", label: "UK Civil Aviation Authority: ATOL protection" }
    guide: deposits-and-payment-protection
  - text: I am paying the company named on the contract, by credit card where possible, not by bank transfer to a personal account.
    source: { url: "https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/using-credit-cards-and-disputing-charges", label: "US FTC: Using credit cards and disputing charges" }
    guide: deposits-and-payment-protection
  - text: The operator has told me what liability insurance it holds, and whether it covers my activities.
    source: { url: "https://www.gov.uk/guidance/foreign-travel-insurance", label: "UK FCDO: Foreign travel insurance" }
    guide: insurance
  - text: My own travel insurance covers medical treatment, evacuation, cancellation and my activities, and starts now.
    source: { url: "https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/before-you-go/your-health-abroad/insurance-coverage-overseas.html", label: "US Department of State: Insurance coverage overseas" }
    guide: insurance
  - text: I have checked my government's travel advice for the destination and the specific areas on my itinerary.
    source: { url: "https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice", label: "UK FCDO: Foreign travel advice" }
  - text: I have an emergency contact in the destination who answers at night.
    source: { url: "https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoid-scams-when-you-travel", label: "US Federal Trade Commission: Avoid scams when you travel" }
    guide: questions-to-ask
  - text: None of the red flags apply (pressure to pay now, payee mismatch, no written terms, unverifiable logos).
    source: { url: "https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/a-z-of-fraud/holiday-fraud", label: "Action Fraud (UK): Holiday fraud" }
    guide: red-flags
---

If any line stays unticked, ask the operator to answer it in writing before you pay. A Trusted Ground listing records what Tourground checked about the company; this checklist covers what only you can check about your own booking.
