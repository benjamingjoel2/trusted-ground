## The short version

Trusted Ground does not set cookies, and does not store anything on your device. You will not see a cookie banner here because there is nothing to consent to.

## How visits are counted

We count page views with Vercel Web Analytics. According to Vercel, it works without cookies: a visitor is identified by a hash created from the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours, and only aggregated statistics are reported to us. Nothing is written to or read from your browser's storage for this purpose. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for what is collected and why.

## Other storage

This site does not use local storage, session storage, fingerprinting scripts, advertising pixels or social media plugins.

## Sites we link to

Company profiles, sources and country reference sites are on other websites. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which apply when you visit them.

## If this changes

If we ever add a cookie or similar technology that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first where the law requires it, and update this page.
