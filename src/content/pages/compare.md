## Payment protection by market

| Market | Main rules for package travel | If the company fails | Card protection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| European Union | Package Travel Directive (EU) 2015/2302 | Organisers must provide security for refunds (Article 17) | Varies by country and card scheme |
| United Kingdom | Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018 | Insolvency protection required; flight-inclusive packages generally ATOL-protected | Consumer Credit Act 1974 s.75 for items over £100 and up to £30,000 |
| United States | No federal package travel law; some states regulate sellers of travel (e.g. California) | No federal scheme | Credit card billing disputes (FTC guidance) |
| Brazil | Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Lei nº 8.078/1990); Cadastur registration for tourism providers | No general insolvency scheme identified | Varies by card issuer |
| Booking direct with an operator abroad | Home-market rules may not apply | Depends on the operator's own country | Depends on how you pay |

## Trade bodies at a glance

| Body | Type | Members | Financial protection scheme? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATTA | Trade association | Adventure travel businesses worldwide | No |
| IAATO | Industry association | Antarctic tour operators | No |
| ASTA | Trade association | Travel agencies and advisors, mainly US | No |
| ABTA | Trade association | UK travel agents and tour operators | Yes, for many packages sold by members |
| ATOL | Licence issued by the UK CAA | UK sellers of flight-inclusive trips | Yes, for ATOL-protected bookings |
