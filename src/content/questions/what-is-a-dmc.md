ADMEI, the trade association for destination management, describes a DMC as a professional services company with local knowledge, expertise and resources that designs and delivers activities, tours, transport and logistics in its destination.

In practice, a DMC is the company that knows which lodge has the better guide, books the permits, and sends the driver. Many DMCs work mostly for overseas agencies and tour operators; some also sell to travellers directly.

On Trusted Ground, each company is labelled **DMC** or **Agency** from the Tourground directory feed. For what the difference means when you book, read [What a DMC is, and how it differs from a travel agency](/guide/dmc-or-agency/).
