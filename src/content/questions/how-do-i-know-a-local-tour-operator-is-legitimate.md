A legitimate operator can prove who it is, and the proof checks out when you look it up yourself.

1. **Registration or licence.** Ask for the number and the issuing authority, then check it on the authority's own website. Brazil's Cadastur register is one example of a public register. See [licences](/guide/licences/).
2. **Memberships.** Confirm any trade association membership in the body's own directory. See [memberships](/guide/memberships/).
3. **Written terms.** A real business has booking conditions, cancellation terms and an itinerary naming its suppliers.
4. **Payment.** The payee should be the company on your contract. Consumer-protection agencies in the US and the UK warn against paying by bank transfer, money-transfer service or cryptocurrency, because these are hard to reverse.
5. **Consistency.** The company name, address and contact details should match across the contract, the website and the register.

The [before-you-pay checklist](/checklist/) puts them on one page.
