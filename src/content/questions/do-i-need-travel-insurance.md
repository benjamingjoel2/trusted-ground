**What the operator's insurance does.** A professional operator should carry liability insurance. It pays if the operator is legally responsible for injury, loss or damage. It does not pay your hospital bill if you fall ill, or refund you if you have to cancel.

**What your own policy should cover.** Check medical treatment and emergency evacuation (often the largest cost in remote areas), cancellation and curtailment, the activities on your trip (altitude trekking, diving and self-drive are often excluded unless declared), and whether it covers the failure of a travel company.

**Why booking locally can make it more important.** If you book direct with an operator outside your home market, the package travel protections of the EU or UK may not apply. Your insurance may be one of the few ways to recover costs if the trip does not go ahead.

Buy your policy when you book, so that cancellation cover starts straight away. See [insurance](/guide/insurance/) in the booking guide.
