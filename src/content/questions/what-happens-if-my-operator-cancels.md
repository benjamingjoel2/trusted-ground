**If your trip is a package sold in the EU.** Article 12 of the Package Travel Directive lets an organiser cancel in limited cases (for example, too few people booked by a deadline set in the contract, or unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances). It must then refund all payments without undue delay and within 14 days. In other cases, you may also be entitled to compensation.

**If your trip is a package sold in the UK.** The Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018 give similar rights.

**If you booked direct with an operator abroad.** Those rules may not apply. What you get back depends on:

- **your contract**: read the operator's cancellation clause before you pay;
- **how you paid**: card payments can be disputed, bank transfers usually cannot;
- **your travel insurance**: some policies cover supplier cancellation or failure, many do not.

**If the operator has failed (gone out of business).** Packages sold in the EU and UK are covered by insolvency protection that organisers must arrange. Outside those markets, card protection and insurance may be your only routes.

Ask the operator before you book: *"What do I get back if you cancel, and how fast?"* Get the answer in writing.
