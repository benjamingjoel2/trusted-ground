**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 30 October 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to parts of Colombia, including all of Norte de Santander (with Cúcuta) and Arauca departments, areas within 5km of the Venezuelan and Ecuadorian borders, much of Chocó and the Pacific coast (including Buenaventura), and parts of Antioquia, Bolívar, Córdoba, Nariño, Cauca, Caquetá, Guaviare, Meta, Putumayo and Vichada. Ask any operator how its itinerary follows current advice.
