**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 20 October 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to parts of Mexico, including Tijuana and Tecate, all of Zacatecas, most of Chihuahua, Sinaloa, Tamaulipas, Michoacán, Colima and Guerrero (with named exceptions such as Mazatlán, Morelia, Manzanillo and Zihuatanejo/Ixtapa by air), parts of Guanajuato and Jalisco, the Guatemalan border area of Chiapas and Federal Highway 199 between San Cristóbal de las Casas and Palenque. We have not yet reviewed a source for the best months; ask any operator which months suit your route, and why.
