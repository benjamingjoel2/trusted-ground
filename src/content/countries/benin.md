**Check travel advice for the north before you book.** As of 4 November 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all travel to Pendjari National Park and its hunting zones, Parc du W, and areas near the Burkina Faso and Niger borders, and against all but essential travel to the rest of the Alibori and Atacora departments. Ask any operator how its itinerary follows current advice, and check your own government's advice and your insurance.
