**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 4 September 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to the parts of Rusizi District within 10km of the border with the Democratic Republic of Congo, including the Rwandan islands in Lake Kivu and Kamembe Airport.
