**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 22 September 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to the Chapare region of Cochabamba Department, including Villa Tunari and highway routes 4 and 24 through Chapare. It also reports a state of emergency, with protests and roadblocks, and notes that altitude sickness is a risk in places including La Paz and the Salar de Uyuni.
