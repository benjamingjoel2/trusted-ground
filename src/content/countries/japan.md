The Japan National Tourism Organization notes that summer is hot and humid with a risk of typhoons in August and September, and that weather varies widely between regions, so check conditions for each place on your route. Golden Week (late April to early May), Obon (mid-August) and the New Year holidays are the busiest travel periods inside Japan.
