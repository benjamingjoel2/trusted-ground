**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 6 November 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to areas within 20km south of the Putumayo River and the Colombian border in Loreto (except the Amazon River and the triple-border area), and to the VRAEM region (the valley of the Apurímac, Ene and Mantaro rivers).
