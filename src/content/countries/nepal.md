The Nepal Tourism Board notes that average temperatures drop about 6°C for every 1,000 metres of altitude. UK travel advice reports that major flooding in August 2026 damaged roads and buildings in Rasuwa district and the Nepal-China border area, and that affected areas may remain unstable, so check conditions on your route.
