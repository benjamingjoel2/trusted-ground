**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 15 July 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all travel to western and central Mindanao and the Sulu archipelago, and against all but essential travel to the rest of Mindanao except Camiguin, Dinagat and Siargao islands. UK travel advice also notes that most typhoons hit between June and November.
