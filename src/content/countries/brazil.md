**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 28 October 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to several river areas in the west of Amazonas State, including the Amazon River and its tributaries west of Codajás, the Itaquaí and Japurá rivers, and the Rio Negro north or west of Barcelos.
