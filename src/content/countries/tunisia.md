**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 24 July 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all travel to the Chaambi Mountains National Park and military zones in the west, the militarised zone south of El Borma and Dhehiba, areas within 20km of the Libyan border north of Dhehiba, and Ben Guerdane, and against all but essential travel to Kasserine Governorate, other areas near the Algerian border and within 75km of the Libyan border (excluding Zarzis and areas north of the C118 road).
