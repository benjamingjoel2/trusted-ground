**Check travel advice before you book.** As of 1 November 2026, the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office advises against all but essential travel to the coastal provinces of Esmeraldas, Manabí, Santa Elena, Guayas (except airside transit at Guayaquil Airport), El Oro, Los Ríos and Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas, and to areas within 20km of the Colombian border, with some exceptions in Carchi. It also reports flooding on Santa Cruz Island in the Galápagos.
